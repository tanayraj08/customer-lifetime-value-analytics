Cohort retention

Share of each first-purchase cohort active N months later (months +1 to +12).

Predicted CLV is a statistical forecast (BG/NBD purchase model x Gamma-Gamma spend model; lifetimes customer_lifetime_value with time=26, freq="W", discount_rate=0.01 - lifetimes counts time in months, see README) for the 4,255 customers with at least one repeat purchase. It is not booked revenue or a guarantee: a calibration/holdout test showed the model ranks customers well (r = 0.80) but over-predicts purchase volume. The 2009-12 cohort contains every customer already active before the data starts, so its retention is inflated. Segment = each customer's RFM segment at the snapshot date.
